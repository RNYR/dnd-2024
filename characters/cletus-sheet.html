Appearance

Ginger hair in a man-bun. A thick red beard down to his belt, tied in knots. Black denim work overalls, black leather Red Wing boots, and a work shirt with sleeves rolled below the elbow over thick, veiny forearms. A pipe carved from oak heartwood is always in his teeth, and a long sharpened stake serves as his walking stick.

Personality

Traits
Gives advice to anyone, on anything, unasked. Quotes The Hobbit for every occasion. Calm in a crisis: he puffs the pipe and gets to work.
Ideal
Work is work. A job worth doing is worth doing right, whether it's splitting cordwood or staking a vampire.
Bond
He owes the Old Lady of the Thicket a debt, every last tree's worth.
Flaw
Can't keep his mouth shut around folks with more power than sense. In Barovia, that's most of them.

Backstory

Born on a dirt-poor farm at the edge of the Big Thicket in East Texas. The only book in the house was his granddaddy's copy of The Hobbit, read aloud every night by the stove. When the farm went under, Cletus took up logging, and he had opinions about how everyone else held their saw.

He felled an oak older than Texas that the old-timers said to leave be. Out of the stump spoke the Old Lady of the Thicket, and instead of killing him she made a deal:

"For every tree you felled, you'll fell a thing that ought not be standing."

She gave him the oak's heartwood. He whittles it into a stake each morning and it is whole again by the next. From the same wood he carved a pipe, lit with a coal from the oak's roots, that has never gone out. She also gave him her voice: the knack for saying the one thing that cuts a body down to size.

One autumn a fog rolled into the Thicket that didn't smell like Texas. "There's a wood out yonder that's sick to the root. Go on, now." He walked in with his stake, his pipe and his book, and walked out in Barovia.
