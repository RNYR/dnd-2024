# The Texan Logger: Warlock (D&D 2024)

> Status: **Step 1 of build, awaiting decisions** (species, background, ability scores)

## Core fantasy
Ex-farmer and logger from Texas with a heavy Southern drawl. Ginger, man-bun, belt-length
beard tied in knots. Black denim work overalls, black leather Red Wing boots, work shirt with
sleeves rolled below the elbow over thick, veiny forearms. Puffs a corncob pipe, hands out snark
and unwanted advice, fights with a single wooden stake used like a dagger, and carries a copy
of *The Hobbit* he has read a thousand times.

## Design goal: three signature strengths
| Trait | Mechanics |
|---|---|
| **The wooden stake** | Pact of the Blade (stake is the pact weapon, uses CHA, can deal Psychic damage), later Thirsting Blade (Extra Attack), Eldritch Smite, Lifedrinker |
| **Snark remarks** | Great Old One subclass (psychic spells, *Dissonant Whispers*, *Tasha's Hideous Laughter*), *Mind Sliver* / *Vicious Mockery* cantrips |
| **Unwanted advice** | GOO **Awakened Mind** telepathy (advice straight into your skull), Healer feat ("rub some dirt on it"), buff spells as "pep talks" |
| *The Hobbit* (bonus) | Hermit background's Book is *The Hobbit*; possible Musician feat (singing the Hobbit songs at camp to grant Heroic Inspiration) |

## Patron hook (draft)
He felled the oldest tree in the East Texas piney woods. Something vast and ancient was asleep in
its roots, and now it whispers in his head. He whispers back to everyone else. The stake is
whittled from that tree's heartwood.
