# The Texan Logger: Warlock (D&D 2024)

> Campaign: **Curse of Strahd (Barovia)**
>
> Status: building step by step, one decision at a time

## Core fantasy
Ex-farmer and logger from Texas with a heavy Southern drawl. Ginger, man-bun, belt-length
beard tied in knots. Black denim work overalls, black leather Red Wing boots, work shirt with
sleeves rolled below the elbow over thick, veiny forearms. Puffs a corncob pipe, hands out snark
and unwanted advice, fights with a single wooden stake used like a dagger, and carries a copy
of *The Hobbit* he has read a thousand times.

## Decisions so far
1. **Patron: Archfey.** Signature combo: a long puff of the pipe, vanish in smoke (Steps of the Fey / Misty Step),
   **Taunting Step** snark at the enemies left behind, reappear and drive the stake (Radiant vs vampires) home.
   Level 6 Misty Escape + Dreadful Step (psychic insults), level 10 Beguiling Defenses (immune to Charm vs Strahd).
   Patron hook: the spirit of the Texas forest he logged dragged him through the Mists as penance.
   The corncob pipe is his spellcasting focus.
2. **Background: Hermit**, a backwoods logger living alone in a cabin. +2 CHA, +1 CON. Healer feat
   (patches allies up while lecturing them). Medicine, Religion, Herbalism Kit. The starting Book is *The Hobbit*.
3. **Ability scores ("Barovia-safe"):** STR 8 (-1), DEX 14 (+2), CON 15 (+2), INT 7 (-2), WIS 11 (+0), CHA 19 (+4).
   Wiry, leathery strength rather than bulk.
4. **Species: Human.** Resourceful (Heroic Inspiration each Long Rest), Skillful (Perception), Versatile (Magic Initiate).
5. **Magic Initiate (Bard):** cantrips *Vicious Mockery* and *Light* (glowing pipe bowl). Level 1 spell: TBD.
6. **The stake:** Pact of the Blade weapon with **Shortsword** stats (1d6 + CHA; Radiant, Psychic or Piercing).
   Also carry a real wooden stake for pinning vampires in their coffins (Curse of Strahd rule).
7. **Skills:** Medicine, Religion (Hermit); Intimidation, Deception (Warlock); Perception (Human).
8. **Warlock cantrips:** *Prestidigitation* (pipe tricks) and *Eldritch Blast* as **"Backdraft Spark"**:
   with the pipe still in his mouth he puffs back into the stem, a curl of smoke rises from the bowl, catches
   a spark, and the spark streaks to the enemy (1d10 force, 120 ft; more sparks as beams scale).
9. **Eldritch Invocations (3):** Pact of the Blade (L1), **Armor of Shadows** (AC 15 in overalls),
   **Devil's Sight** (sees in magical and nonmagical darkness to 120 ft). Plan for L5: Thirsting Blade + Eldritch Smite or Agonizing Blast.
10. **Prepared warlock spells (4):** *Hex* (conc.), *Armor of Agathys* (no conc., cast before fights),
    *Darkness* (conc.; a cloud of black pipe smoke he sees through with Devil's Sight; can't run with Hex),
    *Charm Person* (no conc.; folksy Southern charm, 2 humanoids at a level 2 slot; target knows afterwards).
11. **Starting level: 3** (campaign starts at level 3).

## Patron hook (draft)
He felled the oldest tree in the East Texas piney woods. Something vast and ancient was asleep in
its roots, and now it whispers in his head. He whispers back to everyone else. The stake is
whittled from that tree's heartwood.
